Committees

Three committees.

The committees coordinate the activities of the community: the Discord server and the regular meetings, the GitHub organisation and its data infrastructure, and the joint research activities such as the international round-robin studies. Committee members are volunteers from the community, meetings are open to members, and outcomes are recorded in written minutes.
