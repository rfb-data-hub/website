Why a digital commons?

Bridging the distance.

As in each academic research field, flow battery research is spread over many academic and industrial research teams world-wide. The flow battery commons exists to bridge this distance: one open online community where flow battery researchers can get in contact and discuss important research questions to advance the research field collectively.

    {{#each aimItems}}{{> aim-item}} {{/each}}

These aims reflect the core motivations behind the flow battery commons and guide how the community interacts and collaborates. But everyone is welcome to participate and shape this evolving community in their own way.

Where to find us?

Two platforms.

The flow battery commons has two key online platforms where its members can actively participate: a Discord server for direct communication and a GitHub organization for sharing data, tools, and other digital resources.
